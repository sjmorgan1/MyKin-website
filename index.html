What we do

One managed service, not a box to configure

We specify the right setup for each property, install it in a single visit, and run the platform that watches for change. Nobody on the receiving end has to be technical.

Installed for them

We survey the property, fit everything ourselves, and carry the warranty. Nothing to unbox, pair or configure.

Analytics that notice change

The system builds a picture of someone's normal routine over time, then flags when that pattern shifts in a way that matters.

Consent built in, not bolted on

Oversight is matched to the relationship, from full independence through to full guardianship, with the resident's consent at the centre.

The product

Three levels of support, one system

The hardware and the install are the same throughout. What changes between tiers is the level of oversight, so a home never has to look or feel institutional.

Resident

A smart home with no oversight layer. No one else has access. Built for someone who wants the convenience and safety features with full independence.

Assisted Living

A resident and a carer. The resident keeps full autonomy and decides what is shared, with whom, and when.

Full Guardianship

A guardian-only account for someone who cannot direct their own care. Requires Power of Attorney, with every design decision built around that responsibility.
